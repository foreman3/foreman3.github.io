WELCOME TO THE TABLE SPORTS SOCIETY

A clean strike.
A clever angle.

Move the teal paddle on the left. Hit the ivory puck into the rival’s goal on the right. The copper paddle is your opponent.

Mouse: point on your half. Touch: touch your half and slide; lift to stop. Keyboard: arrows or WASD; hold Shift for fine control. Paddle speed is limited, so sweep into the puck rather than teleporting. Bank shots off the long rails to get around a defender.

Win: reach your “TO WIN” score before the rival reaches its limit. After each goal, a countdown shows whose serve comes next. No match timer: take time to set up a shot. Goals earn 100 points, rail banks add 35 each (up to three), and every unspent rival goal adds 75 when you win. Completed tables bank your points; Retry replays only the current table.

Seven opponents: table 1 is relaxed. Tables 2–5 read, return, and attack more strongly, with room to concede four goals. Table 6 is the mastery test (two goals of margin). Table 7 requires a shutout. All tactics appear by table 5.

P / ? pauses · R starts a new tour · M sound. Touch pause and sound sit above the table; Reset is in the upper right.
