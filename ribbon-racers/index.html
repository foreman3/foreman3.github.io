WELCOME TO THE RIBBON GRAND PRIX

Leave a line.
Close the door.

You're the cream racer, number 1. Everyone leaves a solid ribbon. Outlast every rival by making them run out of room. Don't touch a ribbon, barrier, or edge — even your own trail.

Steer: arrow keys / WASD, or the touch joystick. Turns are at right angles; you cannot reverse. You keep moving after releasing the control.

Brake: hold Space or BRAKE to halve your speed. The gauge refills when released. Slow down to let a rival pass, then cut behind. Release an empty brake to recharge.

Patches: a crash spends a patch, clears nearby ribbon, and gives five moves of protection. Barriers stay solid. Pick a fresh direction quickly. A rival that hits your ribbon earns 1,000 points; other eliminations earn 400. Amber brackets warn of a blocked cell ahead. All features arrive by heat 5; heat 6 tests mastery, and heat 7 has no patches.

P / ? pauses · R restarts all heats · M toggles sound. Retry a heat keeps your earlier score.
