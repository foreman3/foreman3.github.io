FIVE COURTS. ONE CLUB CHAMPION.

Rally Club

A sunlit table, a quick wrist, one more rally.

MOVE with ← → or A / D. On touch, drag the strip below the court. Tap Serve to begin each point.

COUNTER THE SPIN. The ball curves left or right. Hold Q to brush left, E to brush right, or tap a brush button. Brush opposite to the incoming spin as you hit. Right spin? Brush LEFT. Touch selection stays set until you change it.

KEEP IT ON THE TABLE. There are no side walls. A flat or wrong-way stroke carries spin into your return and can send it wide — your shot, your fault. Edge contact aims your return; a clean counterspin earns 25 bonus points.

First to 3, then first to 5. Court 1 always curves right; later courts switch spin and placement. Space serves · P pauses · R restarts · M sound.
