Lift Heist

Collect every red file, then reach the EXIT on the ground floor before the clock runs out.

MOVE
Walk with A / D or ← / →. Stand in either glowing lift and press ↑ / ↓ to change floors. On mobile, slide the joystick or use direction buttons.

DEFEND
Face a guard and press Space or tap STUN. The pulse blocks close shots. Press ↓ away from a lift to crouch under bullets. Contact costs a heart.

SEVEN SHIFTS
Patrols thicken, cameras activate in shift 3, armored guards appear in 4. Shift 5 leaves room to recover; shift 6 tests mastery and 7 demands near perfection.
