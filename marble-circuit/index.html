MARBLE CIRCUIT / SEVEN SUSPENDED COURSES

Find your line.
Feel the momentum.

Roll a glass marble along miniature skyways. Light the three numbered beacons in order, then reach the checkered finish. There are no walls to catch you at the edge.

STEER
Arrows or WASD apply force. Release to coast. On touch, slide the joystick gently for fine control, or choose Buttons in Options.

BRAKE BEFORE TURNS
Hold Space or the BRAKE button to slow down and corner tightly. Release on the straights. Braking is unlimited.

KEEP YOUR PROGRESS
Each beacon is a checkpoint. A fall costs one marble and three seconds, but keeps lit beacons. Five marbles through course 5.

Bumpers arrive in 3, moving sweepers in 4, and striped tailwinds in 5. Brake against a tailwind. Course 5 leaves recovery room; 6 is mastery; 7 needs near-perfect lines. P pauses · R resets the run · M sound.
