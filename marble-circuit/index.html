MARBLE CIRCUIT / SEVEN SCROLLING WORLDS

Find your line.
Feel the momentum.

Roll through seven angled, scrolling worlds. Light three numbered beacons in order, then reach the finish. Blue SCENIC routes are wide and longer; gold EXPRESS routes save time through trickier terrain.

STEER
Arrows or WASD steer in screen directions. Release to coast. Ramps pull you downhill. On touch, use the joystick or choose Buttons in Options.

BRAKE BEFORE TURNS
Hold Space or the BRAKE button to slow down and corner tightly. Release on the straights. Braking is unlimited.

KEEP YOUR PROGRESS
Each beacon is a checkpoint. A fall costs one marble and three seconds, but keeps lit beacons. Five marbles through course 5.

Rival marbles and bubbling acid arrive in 2; get out of acid before it dissolves you. Ride silver rails in 3 and roll into copper cannons in 4. Belts and flashing crushers arrive in 5. Lower decks can catch falls. Brake for rail exits and cannon landings. 6 is mastery; 7 needs near-perfect routing. P pauses · R resets · M sound.
