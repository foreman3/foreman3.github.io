A TIMBER ARCADE · SEVEN FESTIVAL HEATS

Read the bough.
Find your rhythm.

Chop enough logs before the festival bell. Choose the open side of the lowest log. A branch on your side costs a heart and breaks your chain.

LEFT OR RIGHT
← / A chops from the left; → / D from the right. Tap for one stroke, hold to repeat. Touch LEFT and RIGHT with your thumbs. You can switch sides instantly.

LOOK AHEAD
The striped frame marks the log you are cutting. Read the next branches above it. From heat 2, oval knots need two strokes; the first stroke cracks the knot.

MAKE IT SING
Clean cuts grow a score chain. Gold bell-stamped logs arrive in heat 3 and add a second to the clock. Hearts refill each heat. Mistakes discard a log without credit and cost a second. A brief gold ring protects your recovery through heat 6.

Heats 1–5 grow gradually, with room to recover. Heat 6 tests mastery; 7 needs near perfection. P pauses, R starts a new run, M toggles sound. Retry restores this heat’s starting score. Touch Pause and Sound are above; Reset is upper right.
