How to Play

Move with the drag joystick, arrow keys, A/D, or the mouse. Position the paddle, then tap FIRE, press Space, or click to serve.

Build airborne combos, break every destructible brick, and catch colored powerups. Space fires plasma cannons or releases a caught ball.

Armor takes multiple hits, red bricks explode, steel cannot break, green bricks move, and violet bricks phase in and out. Survive all ten sectors and destroy the Reactor Core.

Rotate Device Please turn your phone horizontally for the best Arkanoid experience.
