VELVET COIL

A living necklace. Five locked vaults.
Gather the rubies without tying yourself in knots.

STEER THE COIL
Arrows / WASD. Touch: use the left stick or swipe on the vault. The serpent always moves forward; no reversing.

PLAN YOUR EXIT
Every ruby lengthens your tail. Avoid the walls, gold pillars, and your own body. Follow the tail into open space.

TAKE IT SLOW
Hold Space or SLOW for half speed. Each ruby restores one second of charge. Release to save it for tight turns.

RECOVER & ASCEND
Four lives through vault 4. A crash shortens your coil; gems stay collected. Vault 5 is the mastery test, with two lives.

Reach the gem quota before the clock runs out. Lives and slow charge refill each vault. R resets · M sound · ? help.
