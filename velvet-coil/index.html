VELVET COIL

A living necklace. Five different heists.
Get the rubies. Beat the security. Get out.

STEAL & ESCAPE
Arrows / WASD, left stick, or swipe to steer. Collect the ruby quota, then physically reach the glowing EXIT.

GREED HAS A PRICE
Once the exit opens, up to three teal bonus jewels appear. Each earns 500+ points but adds alarm pressure and costs four seconds. You can leave anytime.

SPEND YOUR TAIL
X / SHED cuts back to six links, costing 25 points per removed link. Your haul stays. It also jams beams briefly. Six-second cooldown.

READ THE SECURITY
Vault 3 aims beams at you: leave the dashed warning line! Vault 4 alternates passages; shutters never crush an occupied doorway. Vault 5 combines both.

Hold Space / SLOW for half speed. Rubies recharge it. Four lives through vault 4; two in vault 5. R resets · M sound · ? help.
