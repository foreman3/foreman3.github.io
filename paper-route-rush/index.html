Paper Route Rush

Ride a street of 24 houses, starting with 8 subscribers. Green DELIVER mailboxes need a paper; gray PASS houses don't subscribe. Papers thrown there are wasted. Miss a subscriber and they cancel. Below 4 customers, your route closes.

Steer toward the correct curb and throw as the mailbox approaches. On mobile, use the left stick and THROW. Dodge traffic and collect paper bundles.
