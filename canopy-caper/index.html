SEVEN GARDENS • FOUR BEETLE ROLES • ONE DIG

Explore the canopy.
Outsmart the patrols.

Rescue every golden seed pod, then reach the peach gate. Each garden has a different shape and grows beyond the screen. The camera follows you; the map shows seeds, beetles, the gate, and your visible area.

MOVE & DIG
Arrows / WASD move and climb. X or Z digs diagonally down in the direction you face. Touch: joystick or direction buttons plus one DIG button. Turn toward soil first; pale stone cannot be dug. Down drops from ropes.

NO MORE STUCK PITS
Holes last eight seconds. If you fall in, a leaf ladder lifts you onto a ledge automatically after two seconds. Hold Up or tap DIG to climb early after 0.8 seconds. Trapped beetles give you time to choose a new route.

READ THE BEETLES
Coral chasers follow nearby movement. Green shield wardens stay near seeds. Purple interceptors occupy vine junctions. Gold striped scouts aim ahead. Change routes and lure them into soil traps; they will not all trail behind you.

Space / WHISTLE calms nearby beetles. Five hearts and two whistles through garden 5; hits keep your seeds. Garden 6 is mastery; 7 permits two hits across sixteen pods and no whistle. All seeds open the gate's pollen refuge. P pauses · R resets · M sound. Retry keeps the garden's starting score.
