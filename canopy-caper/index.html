A CLASSIC CLIMB • COLLECT • TRAP ARCADE

Small gardener.
Big escape.

Rescue every golden seed pod from the beetles, then reach the striped gate at the upper right. Plan a route through the planting shelves: you can climb vines, hang from ropes, and fall safely. Collecting every pod opens the gate's green pollen refuge, which calms beetles within three tiles.

CLIMB & COLLECT
Arrows / WASD move. Up / Down climb vines; Down drops from ropes. Touch: joystick or direction buttons. Walk into golden seed pods to collect them.

DIG A DETOUR
Z digs down-left; X digs down-right. Touch: DIG LEFT / RIGHT. Soil holes refill after 5 seconds. Lure a beetle into one for points and breathing room. Climb Up to leave a hole. Pale stone cannot be dug.

RECOVER & ESCAPE
Space / WHISTLE stuns nearby beetles for 2.5 seconds. Two charges and five hearts through garden 5. Hits preserve seeds and return you to the entrance with 3 seconds of protection. Garden 6 is mastery; 7 allows one hit and no whistle.

P pauses · R resets · M sound. Every garden refills hearts and whistles. Retry restores that garden's starting score. Bright striped beetles move faster from garden 5.
