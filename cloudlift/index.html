HIGH COUNTRY AIR RESCUE

Four seats.
Every life counts.

Fly the yellow helicopter to the numbered rescue pads. Return every hiker to BASE before time runs out. The route strip shows the whole valley.

Fly: arrows / WASD, or the touch joystick. Release to hover. Descend over a marked pad; landing assist cushions your descent. Release to board or unload automatically while still. Four seats mean planning return trips.

Fire: hold Space or FIRE. Shots follow your last left/right direction. Disable flak guns and patrol drones, or fly around their shots. Flashing crosshairs warn where flak is aiming. Hikers cannot be hurt.

Recovery: a hit costs one hull point, grants two seconds of protection, and keeps all passengers. Rough ground bumps you back into the air. Retry restores the current valley and preserves earlier score. Valleys 1–5 leave room for mistakes; 6 tests mastery and 7 requires a clean flight. All hazards arrive by 5.

P / ? pauses · R starts a new rescue · M toggles sound.
