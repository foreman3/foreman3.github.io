Clockwork Blast

Disable every rogue robot, then walk into the striped EXIT. Break crates to open routes and uncover score gears.

WIND & RUN
Arrows / WASD move. Space places a charge. It flashes before bursting in a cross. Turn a corner behind a steel pillar! Two charges can be active.

MAKE ROOM
X spends a three-second shield. Room 2 introduces chain reactions. From room 3, walk into a charge to kick it down the lane. Armored robots in room 5 need two hits.

TOUCH & TIMING
Use the joystick or direction buttons, CHARGE and SHIELD. Five rooms teach the workshop; 6 tests mastery, 7 demands near perfection. R resets; M toggles sound.
