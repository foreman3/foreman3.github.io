A TWELVE-BURROW REACTION ARCADE

Orchard
Watch

The apples are ripe. The marmots have noticed.
Keep the harvest safe, one gentle thump at a time.

THUMP THE THIEVES
Tap or click a striped marmot. On keyboard, use Q W E R / A S D F / Z X C V to match the twelve burrows.

SPARE YOUR CREW
Gardeners wear broad hats and carry a flower. Let them work! Hitting one costs a basket.

PROTECT THE HARVEST
Each escaped marmot costs a basket. Empty taps break your scoring streak, but cost no baskets.

FIVE ROUNDS
Meet each quota before time runs out. Baskets refill each round. Round 2 brings pairs, 3 brings triples, 4 is frantic, and 5 is a five-at-a-time frenzy.

Six baskets give you room to recover through round 4. Pause anytime. Backspace resets · M toggles sound.
