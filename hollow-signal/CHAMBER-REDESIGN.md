# Hollow Signal — chamber redesign, planned before gameplay edits

## Pacing and rules

Aim for roughly **50–80 seconds on a first purposeful clear** of a normal chamber, including traversal, aiming, dodging and equipment use. Skilled return visits should become faster because completed encounter fields remain open. Safe sanctuary rooms deliberately provide shorter rests; guardian rooms combine an approach and a fight rather than inflating the arena. This target is encounter pacing, not a timer that holds a door shut.

Normal horizontal rooms grow from about two screens to eight–ten screens (7,800–9,600 logical pixels). Shafts combine 5,800–6,400 pixels of horizontal exploration with 1,600–2,300 pixels of height. Main exits are placed at the far side; branch lifts occupy separate locations and elevations. Doors cannot all appear in the opening view. Safe entry aprons precede the first challenge.

Most chambers have two or three authored encounter sectors. A sector's containment field is sustained by its marked defenders, not by a key on the exit door. Defeat those defenders to release the field permanently. Other patrols are optional threats. Encounter boundaries are internal terrain, with enemy silhouettes, a defender count and a weakness label. Retreat is possible through the entrance. Clearance persists through death, room changes and reload, so backtracking does not demand repeated combat. A relay after a cleared sector records a local checkpoint; sanctuaries remain the reliable full heal.

**Enemy equipment rules:** ordinary threats accept every weapon. Carapace guards strongly resist small pulses but expose a vulnerable vent during their firing recovery; charged pulses, mines and Seeker can break their armor. Mineral crust guards resist beams and need pulse mines. Phase guards repel physical/pulse weapons and need the Phase beam. Frost disables ordinary/carapace guards and makes a temporary stepping stone; it also damages guards, but is optional throughout the main route. Resistance has visible armor, a letter/symbol, hit feedback and a plain-language hint. Required enemies never demand equipment that cannot yet be acquired on the critical route.

**Bomb jumping:** Spindle blasts launch the folded player without self-damage. Airborne mines can extend the climb. Tap Fire for precise placement, or hold Fire in Spindle to lay a regular chain, while steering over the blast column. Horizontal separation breaks a chain; unfolding stops launches; ceilings stop ascent. Wing does not replace the ball skill, and bomb jumps do not consume its second jump. Teach a short safe ascent at the Nursery, then offer increasingly demanding optional columns in Underroot, Seeker Grotto, Echo Chamber and Witness Garden. No mandatory early jump requires perfect bomb timing.

Keep the existing story, art direction, control bindings, graph and discoveries. Existing saves keep powers, opened doors, guardians, recordings, tanks and sanctuary anchors. Add persistent sector clearances and checkpoint elevation. Expand the escape window to **12 minutes**, reflecting the larger world; restoration/severance and the opened Phase shortcut still determine the return route. Upgrade an in-progress old three-minute escape proportionally when its save is first loaded.
