# Hollow Signal — story planned before implementation

## Premise

Ari Venn arrives at Nacre, a research colony buried beneath the ice of the moon Pelagos. Its last evacuation was twelve years ago. Today its distress signal speaks in the voice of Ari's missing sister, Lio.

The first recordings imply a quarantine disaster. The deeper records reveal that the scientists discovered a vast organism whose mineral nerves could preserve human memory. Nacre's residents joined its Chorus to survive a reactor failure. Lio stayed behind to maintain the link. The corporate quarantine intelligence, the Custodian, now mistakes every visitor for an infection and every preserved resident for corrupted data.

Lio has not been sending Ari orders. Her fragmented memories have been finding their way through the colony's defensive machines. The equipment Ari recovers comes from the same symbiosis. Every new ability is also a piece of the colony's explanation.

## Five acts

1. **Tideglass Ruins — the unanswered return.** A quiet landing, abandoned homes and the first recording establish Ari's personal stake. The resonance lens lets Ari hear sealed machinery. Defeating the Shellkeeper releases a device Lio designed: the Spindle shell, which folds the diver into a small rolling body.
2. **Verdant Underworks — the apparent infection.** Roots have penetrated the habitation systems. The Pulse mine opens scar tissue and brittle construction. The Root Matron's defeat yields the Wing coil. A resident's account establishes that the roots carried food and oxygen when the colony failed.
3. **Choir Archives — the truth preserved.** Vertical halls, frozen memories and the Archive Ray lead to the Phase beam. Lio admits she joined the Chorus voluntarily. The player finds evidence that the Custodian's erasure protocol, rather than the organism, threatens the remaining residents.
4. **Cinder Engine — the cost of keeping a promise.** The system is overheating. Ari follows Lio's failed repair route. The Furnace Eel protects the Thermal mantle, which makes the final descent possible. A recording explains that repairing the archive will leave Lio linked to it; severing it will silence the preserved voices but stop the quarantine machinery.
5. **The Living Heart — rescue defined.** The Custodian combines the colony's defensive systems. After its defeat, Ari reaches the severance console. Restore the Chorus and leave Lio as its willing caretaker, or sever the link and release the colony's distress beacons. Both choices destabilize the facility and require an escape to Ari's ship. Recovered witness recordings add details to the final account.

## Delivery

The opening and four upgrade scenes use brief conversations, then return control. Optional witness terminals fill a journal and support the central reveal. Architecture carries the story: empty houses, roots threading machinery, glass memory vessels, a strained reactor and a warm biological core. The map records places Ari has seen, with readable ability seals and one current objective. The story never advances while the player is reading.

## Ending agency

The player sees both consequences before selecting. Restoration preserves the residents and Lio's chosen role. Severance stops the link and its danger, at the cost of its voices. The final escape is playable rather than a cutscene. Death resumes at the last sanctuary without removing abilities, recordings or defeated guardians.
