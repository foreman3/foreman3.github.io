# Iron Meridian — stage redesign, planned before implementation

The existing illustrated art, controls, saved cores and selectable first four sectors remain. Replace the short repeating approaches with five long journeys. Aim for 2–4 minutes of deliberate first-clear approach play, followed by a substantial guardian fight; practiced movement and the correct arsenal shorten that. There is no timer that holds a gate shut.

## Shared encounter and recovery rules

- Stage lengths: 12,000 / 13,200 / 14,000 / 14,800 / 15,600 pixels. Four defended encounters divide each approach into journeys; their fields release when marked defenders die. Terrain between them varies by stage. Retreatment remains possible before the guardian.
- One midway checkpoint follows the tougher midpoint sentry. It restores armor/energy once and is the sole stage checkpoint. Entering the guardian does **not** heal or move the checkpoint. Death and Continue return to the midpoint, preserving defeated encounter groups and collected caches. Restart starts the stage afresh.
- Two small, deliberately placed optional caches per stage replace supplies on every ledge. One gives +5 armor; the other +25 energy. No routine enemy healing drops. Special energy regenerates at 5/sec, making selective use important; Buster remains unlimited.
- Marked enemies display their recommended weapon and a vulnerable vent after firing. Correct weapons deal substantially more damage and interrupt a salvo; ordinary Buster pellets struggle against plating. Charged Buster during a vent remains a skill route, so selectable sectors never demand an unowned core. Piercing Tidal/Arc and spreading Thorn each solve different formations. Frost slows rushers and breaks reactor armor; Nova is a replay reward.
- Midpoint guardians are larger sentries with a visible tell, a dodgeable burst, a reposition and an exposed recovery. They cannot be bypassed by sprinting, but their field clears permanently for that stage attempt. Final reactor combines several weapon classes.
- Guardian health rises to 160 / 200 / 240 / 280 / 400. Correct weakness multiplies damage; wrong special weapons are resisted, and charged Buster remains the fallback. Bosses have shuffled multi-part attack decks, readable tells, deliberate recovery, broad movement and an overdrive. Difficulty rises across all five sectors rather than peaking at stage three.

## 1 — Copper Harbor, 12,000 pixels

**0–2,000: Salt Pier.** Safe 350-pixel entry, then cargo terraces and a broken wharf. A low lane teaches crouching under high cannon fire. A walker and elevated harbor turret guard the first field at 2,150. Charge breaks its vent without requiring another core.

**2,150–4,500: Shipyard Cut.** Two separated spans with broad islands; cargo walls give a climbable upper crane walkway. One optional armor cache rewards the upper route. Dock drones alternate with floor walkers. A three-defender cargo inspection field at 4,500 stops a blind dash.

**4,500–6,000: Breakwater Battery.** The midpoint sentry, Dockbreaker, has thick plating, a three-shot high pass that can be crouched under, a low return to jump, and a marked pounce. Charge during its vent; Thorn is the replay weakness. Midway checkpoint at 6,100 sits on a quiet broad landing after victory.

**6,100–9,800: Drydock Run.** Cranes lift between staggered decks, a short enclosed cargo lane requires crouch, and water spans require jump/air-dash control. Energy cache lies above a cargo wall. Two armored cannon guards and a moth-like drone defend the final approach field at 9,650. Copper Harbor is the charge/crouch foundation, not an empty opening sprint.

**11,040–12,000: Brass Crab.** Preserve its six roaming patterns, increase durability, tighten its overdrive and add faster low returns. Enter with the armor earned on the approach. Clear tells and longer recovery keep first-core victory possible with Buster; Thorn is much faster on replay.

## 2 — Tempest Spire, 13,200 pixels

**0–2,350: Turbine Promenade.** Wind towers and raised conductor bridges; paired coil sentries let Tidal Disc pierce a line. A vent-timed Buster route remains possible. First field at 2,350.

**2,350–4,950: Broken Skyway.** Wider chasms, moving turbine lifts, and staggered upper balconies. Wind gusts are localized and announced by streamers. Upper armor cache requires climbing a wall. A crossfire formation defends field 4,950; dash between volleys rather than through continuous fire.

**4,950–6,650: Storm Relay.** Midpoint sentry Coil Marshal alternates paired tracking bolts, an aerial vault, and a grounded recovery. Tidal cuts plating and interrupts bolts. Field at 6,450; checkpoint at 6,700.

**6,700–10,900: Lightning Causeway.** Timed discharge columns announce where they will strike. Safe islands and a higher path give routes through them. A deep turbine crossing requires a lift or dash; energy cache is on the upper route. Two coil turrets plus a high drone defend the last field at 10,700.

**12,240–13,200: Volt Kestrel.** Shuffled storm passes, double marked dive, orbiting aimed volleys, and staggered thunder columns. It actually flies to both sides rather than returning to one corner. Tidal is its weakness. An exposed landing gives counterplay after each attack.

## 3 — Glass Garden, 14,000 pixels

**0–2,600: Conservatory.** Curved glass domes, descending planters and thorn beds. Arc Lance pierces insulated stalk guards; without it, vents reward carefully aimed charge shots. First field at 2,600.

**2,600–5,250: Canopy Walk.** Alternating high platforms and lower crouch tunnels create a choice between aerial drones and floor thorns. Upper armor cache is above a wall-climb alcove. Mixed-height stalk formation controls field 5,250.

**5,250–7,000: Pollination Engine.** Midpoint sentry Glass Scythe alternates a ground sweep, marked leap and seed fan; Arc shorts its shell and exposes it. Field at 6,800; checkpoint at 7,050.

**7,050–11,650: Rootworks.** Raised root bridges over thorn beds, planters as broad refuges, and a lift through an abandoned laboratory. Brief vent bursts reward crouching, not waiting forever. Energy cache on a high leaf bridge. An Arc stalk and Thorn-sensitive drone group defend field 11,350.

**13,040–14,000: Thorn Mantis.** Double sickle rush with a delayed reversal; vault across the arena; seed arcs; high/low scythe crossfire. Arc is the weakness. Read its stance before committing a dash; punish the landing rather than standing still and firing.

## 4 — Frost Foundry, 14,800 pixels

**0–2,850: Cold Assembly.** Ice traction, raised machine landings and frost sentries. Thorn's fan controls differently elevated targets. Narrow spike patches are separated by safe braking platforms. First field at 2,850.

**2,850–5,600: Smelter Ruins.** Long safe islands between icy gaps, moving press decks and climbable furnace walls. Armor cache above a sheltered furnace. Two snow turrets and an airborne shard drone guard field 5,600.

**5,600–7,400: Pressure Lock.** Midpoint sentry Cryo Ram slides across its enclosure, marks falling ice, then vents on the floor. Thorn breaks its shell; charge must exploit that brief vent. Field at 7,150; checkpoint at 7,450.

**7,450–12,150: Glacier Conveyor.** Conveyor sections and alternating ice-fall lanes require braking, crouching and well-timed air dashes. Upper route bypasses some ice lanes but brings drones; energy cache rewards it. Fan-sensitive defenders and a charging floor unit guard field 12,000.

**13,840–14,800: Rime Warden.** Two-speed ground slide, marked icicle staircase, ice orbit and paired shockwaves. Thorn is the weakness; terrain remains slippery, so recovery positioning matters. Overdrive reduces gaps while retaining a safe line through each pattern.

## 5 — The Meridian, 15,600 pixels

**0–3,100: Reactor Ring.** Long ring arches and isolated hot grates; mixed Tidal/Arc armor demands weapon switches. First field at 3,100. It tests acquired tools immediately.

**3,100–5,900: Core Transit.** Three broad span crossings with lift and dash alternatives, a low maintenance passage and high drone patrols. Armor cache requires the upper wall route. A formation combining Thorn drones and Frost reactor plating controls field 5,900.

**5,900–7,850: Meridian Sentinel.** Midpoint sentry Prism Sentinel cycles high/low fans, a marked vault and a delayed ground surge. Frost interrupts and slows it. Field at 7,550; checkpoint at 7,850.

**7,850–12,900: Containment Gauntlet.** Announced reactor columns, conveyor landings, crouch channels and two moving bridges. Energy cache above a climbable conduit. Final field at 12,700 pairs Arc/Tidal turrets with a Frost-plated rusher; selecting one weapon for the entire sector is costly. Safe refuges separate combinations.

**14,640–15,600: Sovereign Zero.** Shuffled multi-part reactor orbits, mirrored dashes, delayed targeting columns and high/low crossfire. Below 40% armor it chains a second attack before recovering, with a clear extra tell. Frost is the weakness; its slow has a cooldown so it helps without freezing the fight. Nova unlocks only after victory.

## Implementation and tuning after the plan

The five lengths, four defended encounters, named midpoint sentries, single checkpoints, sparse optional caches, armor counterplay and expanded guardian decks are implemented. Moving lifts are used in Tempest, Foundry and Meridian; Harbor and Garden instead use static crane/root terraces, wall climbs and air-dash routes.

Normal-input tests drove several further passes. The first planned 160-armor guardian punished first-core Buster play too severely, so its final armor is 120 (above the former 88); later guardians retain 200/240/280/400. Charged cores cost 75% of a tap, Frost has a heavy charged hit and projectile interception, and the final overdrive chain has an additional 1.05-second tell. Early enemy salvos deal less damage than later ones. Wall positions were moved away from pit landings; sentry recovery moves continuously rather than snapping home.

The combined approach-and-guardian test exposed spikes overlapping the Garden and Meridian crouch exits. Those beds now start beyond the tunnels with space to jump; a geometry regression prevents recurrence. Conveyor motion now participates in solid collision so releasing movement cannot carry the player through a closed encounter field.

All five complete sector runs now pass with normal inputs, damage and the appropriate acquired cores. Harbor used its midway retry; Meridian finished with 2 armor. Buster-only comparisons take much longer or exhaust the three attempts. Full desktop/mobile regressions, five illustrated landscape reviews, boss pattern motion, checkpoint recovery and real-time browser checks were repeated. Current evidence and test scope are recorded in README.md and the adjacent JSON reports.
