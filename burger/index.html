How to Play

Assemble towering burgers while dodging mischievous food foes.

Drag the joystick to move and climb. Ladders gently pull you into position; slide sideways near a landing to step off. Tap PEP to unleash pepper spray.

Walk across all three glowing pressure points on an ingredient to drop it. Carry an enemy for one extra floor, collect condiments, and complete every burger stack.

How to Play

Assemble towering gourmet burgers while dodging mischievous food foes. Use the drag joystick or Arrow Keys to move and climb, and tap PEP or Space to unleash a burst of pepper spray that stuns anything in front of you. Ladders magnetize gently when you climb, and a sideways press near a landing steps off cleanly. Walk across all three pressure points on each ingredient, carry enemies for an extra floor, and complete every mega stack!
