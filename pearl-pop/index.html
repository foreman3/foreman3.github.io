A DEEP-SEA BUBBLE SHOOTER

Make room for wonder.

Aim a pearl into two or more matching pearls. Connected groups of three pop; pearls hanging below them fall for double points. Clear the reef to advance.

Desktop: move mouse and click, or ← → / A D and Space. Q swaps the current and next pearls.

Touch: drag anywhere on the reef to aim, then tap FIRE. SWAP changes your pearl. Release your finger to keep your aim.

The dotted guide shows your first contact. Side walls bank shots. Every pearl has its own symbol as well as a color.

No timer. Watch your shot supply. Consecutive non-matches raise the tide; don't let pearls cross the dotted danger line. A match resets that counter. Retry refills the current reef.

Seven reefs: 1 teaches, 2–4 build, 5 tests your choices with recovery room. 6 is mastery; 7 asks for near-perfect efficiency.
