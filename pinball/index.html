Neon Nova

Left flipper: Left Arrow or Z
Right flipper: Right Arrow or /

Hold Space or Enter, then release to launch. Follow the lit objective: spell NOVA, clear the target bank, charge the three bumpers, rip the Ion Spinner, then shoot the lit jackpot. Drop all four WARP guards to open the center portal and blast toward an unlit NOVA lane.

Each jackpot raises the reactor level and the next jackpot value. Every second reactor level awards an extra ball. Watch the dangerous outside lanes.

Press R after game over. Three balls. Build the reactor.
