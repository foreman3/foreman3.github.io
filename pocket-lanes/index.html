WELCOME BACK TO THE LANES

Ten frames.
Make them yours.

Bowl a full game with standard ten-pin scoring. Strikes earn your next two rolls; spares earn your next roll. The tenth frame grants the usual bonus balls. The maximum is 300.

Choose your equipment. Straight holds its line, Gentle Curve rolls a smooth arc, Strong Hook sweeps harder, and Late Snap saves its sharp turn for the back end. Switch balls between shots; choose right or left hand to reverse the hook.

Read the lane. Easy, Medium or Hard selects a new random oil pattern. It stays invisible and consistent for all ten frames. Oil makes the ball slide; dry boards let it grip. Watch your shots and adjust.

Focus, then time your roll. Drag toward your chosen line, or use START and FOCUS. The brass ring marks your focus; the bright ring sweeps around it. ROLL captures the moving aim when you release the button (or press Space). Slow shots sweep narrowly and gently. Faster shots sweep wider and quicker. Watch the marker, then take your shot.
