WELCOME TO POCKET LANES

A little hook.
A lot of heart.

Three racks. Ten pins each. Two rolls per rack to reach the league's pin target. A strike finishes the rack; otherwise, pick up the spare.

Aim: drag from near the foul line toward the pins, or use START and AIM. The dotted line shows your current release direction. From league 2 it sways gently: time your ROLL, or compensate with AIM. Aim beside the front pin to spread the impact.

Hook: the HOOK slider bends the ball after the oil ends. Long oil bends later; short oil bends earlier. Start with a straight ball. Press ROLL when ready. There is no deadline. Take time to line up.

Keyboard: ← → aim · A / D starting point · Q / E hook · Space roll / next rack · P pause · R new run · M sound.

League 1 has bumpers. 2 introduces hook; 3 introduces different oil lengths. All features arrive by 5. Leagues 1–5 allow missed pins and spare recovery. 6 is a mastery test; 7 asks for 29 of 30 pins. Retry any league while keeping earlier score.
