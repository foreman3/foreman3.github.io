Trap the Mouse

Plant a hedge on an empty plot. The mouse takes one step toward the edge.

Follow its tracks and close every way out. Hold a plot to scout your move. Lift to cancel.

Use the arrows to choose a plot, hold P to scout, and press Enter to plant. U undoes a move.
