Barrel Brigade

Climb the crooked girders, leap the runaway barrels, and ring the shift bell. Four hard hats give you room to learn.

MoveArrow keys or WASD. Up and down climb ladders.
JumpSpace clears barrels. Clean leaps score a bonus.
UtilityR restarts the level. M toggles workshop sound.
MobileUse the stick to move and climb; tap Jump to vault.
