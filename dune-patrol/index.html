A ROVER ARCADE · SEVEN DESERT CROSSINGS

Keep your wheels
off the cracks.

Your rover drives itself. Jump the dark fissures and shoot the rocks. Reach the expedition flag at the end of each route.

CHOOSE YOUR ARC
Space / ↑ / W jumps. Tap for a quick hop; hold for a long jump. Release before the next jump. High arcs collect optional survey diamonds from route 2. On touch, hold JUMP with your left thumb.

KEEP FIRING
Hold X / F or FIRE. Your twin guns shoot forward and upward. Rocks arrive in route 2, bomb-dropping aircraft in 3, linked gaps in 4, and two-hit armored aircraft in 5.

RECOVER & REPEAT
A hit costs one hull section and rescues you onto the road with brief protection. Four sections refill each route through 5. Accurate jumps and clean shots build a score chain.

Routes 1–5 build gradually with room for mistakes. Route 6 tests mastery; 7 needs near-perfect timing. P pauses, R starts a new run, M toggles sound. Touch Pause and Sound are above; Reset is upper right. Retry repeats the current route and restores its starting score.
