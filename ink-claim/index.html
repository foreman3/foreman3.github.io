INK CLAIM

Finish the poster before the ink moths spoil it. Move on the printed edge, then tap DRAW and cross blank paper. Connect to any printed area to claim the enclosed space.

MOVE
Arrow keys / WASD. On touch, use the left control.

DRAW
Tap Space or the right DRAW button to arm the pen. Tap again to cancel before starting a line.

WATCH THE LINE
A moth touching an unfinished line costs a pen. The line also dries out if you take too long.
