A BALLOON ARCHERY ARCADE · SEVEN WATCHES

A little fox.
A very big watch.

Wolves are floating into Blackberry Hill! Move your cable lift up and down and shoot the balloons above their heads. Lead a descending balloon: your arrow takes time to cross the clearing.

TAKE THE SHOT
Arrows / W S move. Hold Space to shoot. On touch, slide the left strip to position your lift and hold SHOOT with your other thumb.

READ THE RAID
Wolves throw stones from watch 2: shoot them out of the air. Shielded balloons (3) open and close. Wind (4) bends arrows. Patched balloons (5) need two hits.

BUY SOME SPACE
X / HORN clears stones and opens shields, freezing nearby wolves for 3 seconds. Two uses through watch 5. An escaped wolf steals one pantry basket; stone hits also cost one. Damage grants 2 seconds of stone protection.

Reach the pop goal and finish the raid with a basket remaining. Pantry and horns refill every watch. Watches 1–5 build from gentle to demanding with room to recover; 6 tests mastery, 7 asks for near-perfect shooting. P pauses, R resets the whole run, M toggles sound. Touch pause and sound stay in the header; Reset is at the upper right.
