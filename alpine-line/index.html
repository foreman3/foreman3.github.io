A CLASSIC DOWNHILL SLALOM ARCADE

Find your Alpine Line.

Carve between each numbered flag pair. Reach the finish with enough gates and at least one heart. Missing a gate breaks your streak; hitting an obstacle costs a heart.

STEER & CARVE
← → or A / D steer. Release to straighten. Trees and rocks have smaller hit boxes than their artwork. Clean gate streaks build your score.

SLOW TO RECOVER
Hold Space or ↓ to brake. Your reserve refills when released. Rough blue snow reduces grip; striped moguls slow you. Crossing riders appear on run 4.

SEVEN DESCENTS
Touch: joystick or direction buttons, plus BRAKE. Linked turns arrive on run 5. Runs 1–5 allow missed gates; 6 tests mastery and 7 needs near perfection. P pauses, R resets, M toggles sound.
