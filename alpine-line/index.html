A CLASSIC SLALOM TIME TRIAL

Race your Alpine Line.

Reach the finish before the qualifying time. Every missed gate adds 2 seconds to your total. Make the time and you unlock the next race—even with missed gates.

CARVE YOUR LINE
← → or A / D steer. Fast skis make wider turns. Brake before tight changes of direction, then accelerate out of the turn.

CONTROL SPEED WHILE RACING
Hold ↑ / W to accelerate; ↓ / S or Space to brake. Release both to settle toward normal speed. No reserve and no speed selection before the start.

FEEL THE CLOCK
Touch: steer with the joystick or direction buttons; hold ACCELERATE or BRAKE. The pace display estimates your finish at your current downhill speed. Later races add more gates and faster turns. P pauses; R resets; M toggles sound.
