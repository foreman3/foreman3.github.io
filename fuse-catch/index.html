A KABOOM-STYLE CATCHING ARCADE

Fuse Catch

Bombs arc away from the rooftop. Watch their motion and choose which falling threat to intercept first.

MOVE
Use A / D or ← / → on desktop. Slide the joystick or use left/right buttons on mobile. Catch with the cart rim.

RESCUE
Each miss costs a bucket. Press Space or tap WATER to clear nearby low bombs. Charges refill each shift.

SEVEN SHIFTS
Double drops arrive in shift 2, quick fuses in 4, and blue bombs reverse direction midway in 5. Shift 6 tests mastery; shift 7 demands near-perfect routing.
