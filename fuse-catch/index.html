Fuse Catch

Roll the safety cart below the rooftop dropper. Catch each lit bomb before it reaches the street.

MOVE
Use A / D or ← / → on desktop. Slide the joystick or use left/right buttons on mobile.

SAVE A MISS
Each uncaught bomb costs one bucket. Press Space or tap WATER to sweep up low bombs; charges refill each shift.

SEVEN SHIFTS
Catch the posted quota. Drift arrives in shift 3, quick fuses in 4, and zigzag bombs in 5. Shift 6 tests mastery; shift 7 asks for near-perfect catching.
