Operator’s manual

Qbert Clockwork

Hop onto every tile to light the pyramid. Each hop ticks the clock. On the chime, the purple winders move to the tiles marked ×.

Tap a neighboring tile or use Q E / A D for the four diagonals. The side lifts return you to the top. One rewind per movement can take back a mistake.
