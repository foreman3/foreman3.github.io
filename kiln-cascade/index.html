Kiln Cascade

Arrange falling columns of three ceramic tiles. Match three or more identical symbols across, down, or diagonally. Fired tiles disappear; falling tiles can chain into another match.

← → / A D move. ↑ / W cycles the three tiles. ↓ / S lowers; Space drops. Touch players use the labeled buttons.
