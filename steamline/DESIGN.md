# Steamline: the city puzzle campaign

The player's feedback supersedes the old successful speed-controller benchmarks. The interesting problem is building a long, working route. The revision removes automatic boiler starts, dwindling lives, countdowns, speed bonuses and punishment for experiments from all fifty levels.

Plan without a clock. Test a route when ready. A failed test highlights its first problem and retains every fitting. Return to the plan at any point, undo freely, and repeat. Completing a puzzle unlocks the next; earlier boards remain available. Stars reward economical builds and a clean bench, never reaction speed, attempts or hint avoidance.

Ten five-level districts introduce one idea at a time. Introductory levels of each district ease the combination burden before later levels combine ideas.

| Levels | New puzzle idea | Decision it creates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–5 | Route length and anchored fittings | Find room for a detour rather than taking the shortest path. |
| 6–10 | Service rings | Choose an efficient visiting order. |
| 11–15 | One-way check valves | Approach a compulsory fitting from the correct side. |
| 16–20 | Limited straight/elbow stock | Balance turns against long runs; lifting refunds stock. |
| 21–25 | Overpass bridges | Cross the same square once on each independent axis. |
| 26–30 | Paired underground ports | Route into an entrance and continue from its distant exit. |
| 31–35 | Switch-operated gates | Visit a switch before approaching its gate. |
| 36–40 | Heater and heat range | Build length before reheating, then reach the receiver while hot. |
| 41–45 | Heat-consuming radiators | Reserve enough heat for deliveries as well as the remaining route. |
| 46–50 | Ordered deliveries | Visit numbered rings in order while combining the learned mechanisms. |

Keep the existing eight-by-six board and brass workshop character. Every board is deterministic and has a validated construction witness; different routes are accepted if they meet the same constraints. Fifty levels use different paths, endpoint positions, anchors, obstructions and combinations rather than fifty speed settings. Crossings and portals are real graph operations, not decorative markers. All required features are explicit objectives.

The save contains unfinished boards for each visited level, unlocked level, best stars and fitting counts, current tools and recent undo history. Save after edits and completion, and on leaving. Reload returns to a safe, untimed planning state, retaining the whole board; flow demonstrations can be replayed. Storage failures are visible. Downloadable backups and validated import allow moving between browsers; importing requires a concrete confirmation and backs up the previous save.

Verification must construct and actually simulate all fifty witness routes, independently reject violations of every new rule, check varied board layouts and each new mechanic in the browser, exercise real keyboard/touch controls and persistence across reload/new browser contexts, inspect the required responsive sizes, and preserve unrelated Marble Circuit work.
# Working municipal boiler

The Blender-created boiler becomes a read-only workshop instrument. Planning remains untimed. Test Flow opens its wheel, illuminates its pilot/firebox and emits a small amount of steam. Pause, help and ledger browsing freeze it. A blocked test returns it to idle with the layout preserved; success holds the warm state. In heat contracts its needle and numeric caption follow the current visited square's heat, including heater refills and radiator costs. Earlier contracts explicitly identify it as a flow indicator.

Four transparent, front-orthographic body/light/wheel/needle images use calibrated source pivots; no 3D engine is necessary. The tiny board glyphs retain their readability. Desktop rack, short-screen side margin and portrait lower instrument layouts keep controls and the board clear. Reduced motion removes decorative animation and needle tweening. Campaign rules, fifty boards and save format are unchanged.
