A PIPE DREAM ARCADE · SEVEN CONTRACTS

Keep the city warm.

Build a continuous pipe from the BOILER → to the → RECEIVER before steam reaches an open end. Visit every numbered service ring and meet the pipe-run target.

01 · FIT
Choose a straight or elbow, rotate it, then tap an empty square. Tap a dry pipe to turn it. Wet pipes stay fixed. To replace a dry pipe, select LIFT PIPE and tap it.

02 · ROUTE
Rings can be visited in any order. From contract 4, fixed arrow valves must be entered from the tail and exited through the point.

03 · RECOVER
A leak spends a repair seal and holds steam for 5 seconds. Repair the open end. HOLD buys 4 more seconds; two uses through contract 5.

Desktop: arrows/WASD move the cursor; 1/2 choose; X rotates; Space fits or turns; Backspace removes a dry pipe; V holds steam; Enter opens the boiler early for bonus points; press again for fast flow ×3 when your route is ready. P pauses, R resets, M toggles sound. Touch: tap the rack, rotate and board. Pause and sound are above the board.

Contracts 1–5 build from forgiving to demanding with three repair seals. Contract 6 is mastery. Contract 7 has no seals or holds: a near-perfect run.
