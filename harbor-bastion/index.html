A PERSISTENT FORTRESS CAMPAIGN

Build your Harbor Bastion.

One castle survives thirteen sieges. Walls, damage, buildings, health and supplies carry forward. Your first battle starts with a bare keep and one cannon.

DEFEND
Click or hold where a ship will be when your shots arrive. White shots are yours; red shells are hostile. A shell strikes the first wall or building in its path. Only a visible KEEP HIT reduces keep health. Shoot shells down, or later build a magician. There is no instant repair during battle.

BUILD BETWEEN BATTLES
Construction is untimed. Choose one of three wall pieces in PLANS; rotate and fit it entirely on empty land. No overlap. Touch: drag the preview, then tap PLACE. Three small patch stones help fill awkward holes. Undo is free before battle; salvage returns some supplies.

EXPAND & UPGRADE
Upgrades must fit inside a closed wall area. Tower at 3, larger keep at 5, magician at 7, captain at 9, workshop at 11. Breaches do not switch buildings off; direct hits can disable them. Repair buildings through PLANS. R / ↻ retries this level from its preparation checkpoint. Your castle saves after victories and construction.

CONSTRUCTION IS UNTIMED
