A CLASSIC FORTRESS ARCADE

Hold your Harbor Bastion.

Seven coasts. Two jobs: seal your fort with wall pieces, then sink every raider before they breach the keep.

1 · BUILD
Move the preview over broken walls. Click to place; X rotates. Pieces may overlap existing walls, but must add a stone. Seal every gap, then choose TO BATTLE. Extra enclosed land earns points.

2 · DEFEND
Click where a ship will be when your cannonball lands. Hold to keep firing. Cannonballs take time to travel; red enemy shells can be shot down. E / MEND restores the fort and clears incoming shells, twice per coast through level 5.

KEYBOARD & TOUCH
Arrows / WASD move the cursor; Space places or fires. Touch: drag the preview, then tap PLACE; tap or hold on the sea to fire. ROTATE and MEND are beside the board. P pauses, R resets, M toggles sound. Retry a coast freely. All mechanics appear by 5; 6 tests mastery; 7 demands precision.
